05
Principles

The honest part

What the system will not do matters as much as what it will. These three rules are enforced in code, not promised in copy.

Rule 01

A low-confidence scan is never dressed up as a diagnosis.

The scanner answers on about seven photographs in ten and says it is undecided on the rest, because a confident wrong name costs a farmer a spray they did not need.

Rule 02

A number the farm did not measure is labelled as what it is.

Simulated sensor readings are marked SIMULATED wherever they appear, estimates are marked estimated, and a farmer's own typed figures are marked as theirs. Where a service cannot be reached, the page says so and says why.

Rule 03

The assistant answers farming questions, and nothing else.

It will not invent a mandi price, a fertiliser dose or a pesticide name. If the figure is not in front of it, it says it will check rather than guessing.

The fifteenth reads what the other fourteen produced and validates it before any of it is spoken or shown. Each reports its own confidence, and an agent with nothing solid to say says so.

Soil from ISRIC SoilGrids, live weather from Open-Meteo, mandi prices from the Agmarknet feed, and maps from OpenStreetMap — plus the farm's own 7-in-1 sensor when one is connected.
