    Your voice

    1. Saarthi shows you three short sentences.
    2. You read each one aloud while it records.
    3. It checks each recording and says if one needs doing again.

    A voice match helps Saarthi know it is you. It is not a password: it never opens your account, authorises money or unlocks your data on its own, and two similar voices can be confused. Only numbers are stored - no recording is kept, and nothing can be played back.
