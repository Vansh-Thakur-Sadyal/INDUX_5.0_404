Good establishment decides the ceiling of the crop. Nothing later fully compensates for
a thin or uneven stand.

Seed:
- Use certified or truthfully labelled seed of a variety recommended for the agro
  climatic zone, with a known germination percentage and recent test date.
- Adjust seed rate for germination percentage, seed size, sowing date and method. Late
  sowing usually calls for a higher rate to compensate for reduced tillering.
- Seed treatment protects against seed-borne and early soil-borne problems, and
  rhizobium or PSB inoculation helps legumes. Follow label order when combining
  treatments.

Sowing:
- Sow into adequate soil moisture at a uniform depth. Too deep delays and weakens
  emergence, especially in small seeded crops.
- Line sowing with a seed drill gives even spacing, correct depth and easier weeding
  compared with broadcasting.
- Correct row spacing balances canopy closure against air movement and access.

Conditions to check before sowing: forecast rainfall in the next few days, soil
temperature for the crop, residual herbicide from the previous crop, and field
drainage. Sowing immediately before heavy rain risks crusting and poor emergence.
