A farming companion that listens. Questions

Asked often enough

How do you price a project?

Fixed price for a scoped build, monthly for embedded or ongoing work. Either way you see the number and what it covers before anyone starts.

Is our project too small?

Almost certainly not. A single page that saves one person an hour a day is a good project, and it is usually the fastest one to prove out.

Who owns the code?

You do — repository, containers, credentials and all. Nothing we build for you should be difficult to take somewhere else.

How soon do we see something?

Something you can click inside the first couple of weeks on most projects, and sooner than that on small ones.

Will you work in our stack?

Usually, yes. Where we would choose differently we will tell you why, and then it stays your call.

Can you take over something half built?

Often. We start by reading it end to end and telling you honestly what shape it is in — before anyone commits to a plan.

Got something you want built?

Tell us what you are trying to do and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right team for it.
